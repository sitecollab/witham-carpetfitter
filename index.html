About

11 years fitting floors, no showroom in sight

Mark Doel spent nine years fitting carpets for a regional flooring retailer — the kind with a showroom and a sales team — before deciding customers deserved to deal with the person actually doing the work, not a rep upselling underlay and finance plans.

Since 2015, Witham Flooring Co. has fitted carpet, vinyl and LVT across Witham and the surrounding villages, with samples brought to your home and no showroom overhead to pay for.

Mark works alongside one regular fitting partner, so jobs don't wait on holiday or sickness — but the person who measures your room is still the one who turns up to fit it.
